## Bounded GPU capture
- Activation uses the existing `enableDebugOptions` flag. Verified client `config/embeddium-shaders.properties` already has `enableDebugOptions=true`, `enableShaders=true`, `shaderPack=ComplementaryUnbound_r5.9.zip`; no config or shaderpack edits were made.
- On the first world frame of each pipeline: 15 seconds warmup, then 30 seconds capture. Timestamp scopes are sampled every fourth frame; resource counters cover every frame in the capture window. Timing windows use monotonic wall time, not FPS assumptions.
- GL 3.3 / ARB_timer_query and nonzero timestamp counter bits are required; unsupported contexts log a disabled message. Up to 512 timestamp pairs (1024 query IDs, allocated lazily), never reuse pending/open pairs. Poll only at subsequent frame boundaries, at least three frames later; check both results' AVAILABLE before reading either 64-bit result. Saturation drops scopes instead of blocking. No glFinish, pixel readback, or wait loop in the profiler.
- Cumulative `[GpuProfile]` summaries every five seconds show capture frames, sampled frames, dropped scopes, pending pairs and counters. Timing rows contain the actual program name, GPU mean/max milliseconds and resolved sample count, sorted by mean (top 24 on progress, up to 256 named aggregates in the final report).
- Stop submitting at 45 seconds; drain available results until empty or 50 seconds, then report `complete` and delete all queries, including unresolved ones. The timeout is checked on render frames: if rendering is suspended, cleanup occurs on resume or pipeline destroy. Destroy/reload reports partial results and deletes queries; a new pipeline rearms a fresh capture. Completed cached pipelines do not continually rearm.
- Timers: individual begin/prepare/deferred/composite raster programs; named compute dispatches in these chains and final; generated mipmaps named by requesting raster program (final separately); final draw or copy; history materialization; shadow renderer total; three real gbuffer intervals delimited by begin/shadows/prepare/deferred/composite boundaries.
- Gbuffer/shadow intervals are inclusive GPU timestamp intervals, not CPU timers or pure shader active-cycle measurements. They can include GPU idle gaps and nested history operations. Do not sum inclusive parents and children. Mipmap/history mean is per operation, not per frame; use samples and sampledFrames to interpret frequency. No complete-frame total or per-gbuffer-program breakdown is claimed; setup/shadow computes, explicit barriers, clears, depth copies and color conversion are not separately profiled.
- Counters: mip requests/generated/skipped across composite and final; history publish calls, actual copy calls, alias sampler resolutions, eager publishes for permanently untracked/ineligible targets, mip-triggered materializations and pending untracked-access fallbacks. Alias reads count CPU sampler resolutions, not shader texel fetches. Eager publishes capture persistent fallback even when its cause was established at construction. Counts are not estimates from GL queries and are not measured FPS gains.
- Existing debug diagnostics remain unchanged and can themselves perturb rendering; warmup excludes their initial quad samples, but other debug activity, streaming, animation, power/clocks and profiler overhead remain live-test limitations.
